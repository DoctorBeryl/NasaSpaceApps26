The side panel

A HUD that stays
out of your way.

Health data sits in a translucent side panel at the edge of your vision. The center of view stays clear for the task you're doing, and VEGA brings a reading forward only when it matters.

  • Glanceable vitals with live sparklines and color-coded status.
  • Dock left or right, or hide the panel completely by voice.
  • Priority alerts take over the view only for critical readings.
  • Command log keeps a record of every interaction for the flight surgeon.

Meet VEGA

Vital Evaluation &
Guidance Assistant

VEGA is a compact language model trained on space-medicine protocols. It runs entirely on the glasses, so it keeps working through communication blackouts with Earth. It understands natural speech, sends each request to the right diagnostic tool, and answers out loud.

CrewVEGA, I feel a bit lightheaded.
VEGAYour SpO₂ is 97% and steady, but your heart rate is 14 bpm above your baseline. Cabin CO₂ is slightly high. I'd suggest a short break and some water. Want me to start a breathing exercise?

How it works

From sensor to sentence.

ICON_SENSE

Sense

Optical, thermal, pressure and IR eye-tracking sensors in the frame sample the wearer's body continuously.

ICON_THINK

Interpret

VEGA compares each reading against the crew member's own baseline and the habitat's telemetry.

ICON_SPEAK

Respond

Results show up on the side panel and VEGA speaks them through bone conduction. Nobody has to tap anything.
