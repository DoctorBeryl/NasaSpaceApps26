Crew sign-in

Look into the lens.

AstraSight recognizes who's wearing it, so VEGA reads your vitals against your own baseline. There's no password to type with gloves on, unless you'd rather use one.

  • Face scan. Look at the lens and turn your head slightly. New crew enter a name first; it takes about ten seconds.
  • Stays on this device. Face data is stored locally and is for demo purposes only. Camera images never leave your browser.
  • Email and password is a standard Firebase account, if you'd rather not scan your face.
