    Why the crew needs this

    On a Mars transit, a message to Earth takes up to 22 minutes each way. The crew medical officer has to work through a problem alone, and IMCA gives that work a structure.

    What it builds on

    NASA and Google are testing the Crew Medical Officer Digital Assistant for the same job. IMCA runs MedGemma, an open medical model, on the ship's own computer, so it works with no link to Earth.

    Where the AI stops

    • Red flags and escalation are fixed rules, not AI.
    • ASTRA lists causes to consider, never a diagnosis.
    • Medicines and doses are the flight surgeon's call.
