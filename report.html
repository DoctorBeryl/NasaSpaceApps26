    06 / SUIT CHECK · SENSOR HEALTH

    EVA SUIT CHECKOUT

    ASTRA watches every sensor in every suit, not just the readings. A suit with a failed or untrustworthy life-support sensor (marked LS) is NO-GO: its wearer can't leave through the airlock until the sensor is replaced or they swap to a suit that passes. A fault in a health sensor alone gives GO · LIMITED: the EVA can go ahead, but ASTRA can't watch that reading.

    DEMO · BREAK A SENSOR

    FAULT INJECTION

    Simulates a broken sensor so you can watch the checks catch it. Detection times are shortened for the demo.

    HOW ASTRA CHECKS A SENSOR

    FAULT DETECTION RULES

    • No signal: no data from the sensor for 5 s.
    • Impossible reading: a value no working sensor can give, such as 130 % O₂.
    • Stuck: the value hasn't changed at all for 6 s. Real sensors always jitter a little. A real suit would wait longer.
    • Disagreement: the primary and backup O₂ sensors differ by more than 3 %. If only one is outside its normal range, that one is blamed.
    • Noisy: readings jump far more than the sensor's normal jitter.
    • Out of checkout range: a suit on the rack should read inside a known band. Skipped while the suit is outside on an EVA, where readings really change.
    • Calibration overdue.
