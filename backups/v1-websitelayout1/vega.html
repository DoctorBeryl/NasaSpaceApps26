The language model

VEGA
Vital Evaluation & Guidance Assistant

VEGA is a small, specialised language model built for one job: understanding what a crew member says about their health and acting on it right away. It runs on the glasses, works with no link to Earth, and was tuned on space-medicine protocols.

Design principles

Built for life at 400 km.

Works offline

Mars missions can face communication delays of more than 20 minutes. VEGA never relies on a ground link to understand a command or answer it.

Personal baselines

VEGA judges each reading against the wearer's own history, not population averages, so it catches changes earlier.

Clear view first

Answers are short and spoken. Visuals stay on the side panel, and only critical alerts are allowed into the center of view.

Knows its limits

VEGA flags readings and makes suggestions but never diagnoses. Anything serious goes to the flight surgeon along with the data behind it.

Hands-free

The glasses can be fully operated in gloves or during EVA. Every action is available by voice.

Calm tone

Even warnings are delivered in a steady voice, with one clear action to take first.
